## About Us

WTechX AI & Robotics is a PhD-led AI and robotics engineering company. We turn peer-reviewed research into systems that work in the real world — on construction sites, in factories, and in the field.

Founded in **2022**, WTechX is headquartered in **Karachi, Pakistan**, with a presence in **Tokyo, Japan**, serving businesses worldwide.

## What We Do

- **Robotics Engineering & Physical AI** — Localization, SLAM and LiDAR 3D mapping, perception, sensor fusion and autonomy for robots and industrial machines.
- **Gaussian Splatting & Digital Twins** — Photorealistic 3D capture and digital twins of buildings and sites, with deliverables agreed and validated per project.
- **AI Agents & Workflow Automation** — Custom AI agents that work with business data, tools and real operational workflows.
- **AI & Robotics R&D** — Feasibility studies, benchmarking and prototyping grounded in peer-reviewed research.

## Core Technologies & Expertise

**Robotics & Perception:** ROS / ROS 2 · LiDAR-inertial SLAM · Robot perception · Sensor fusion & calibration · Visual-inertial navigation · Autonomous navigation

**3D Mapping & Reconstruction:** LiDAR 3D mapping · Gaussian Splatting · Digital twins · 3D reconstruction · PCL

**AI & Development:** Computer vision · Machine learning · AI agents · AI workflow automation · Gazebo simulation
